Keep this in step with the app: if a feature or the privacy policy changes, update it here too.

Graphics in this folder:

- `icon-512.png`: the 512 × 512 app icon
- `feature-graphic.png`: the 1024 × 500 feature graphic

- `screenshots/`: five 1080 × 1920 phone screenshots (Today in Paper and Night, Browse, Learn,


**Full description** (4000 max)

```
Kural Daily brings you one Thirukkural each day: Thiruvalluvar's couplet set on its two original lines, with an English translation and a clear meaning in Tamil (Mu. Varadarasanar) and English.

EVERY DAY
• A new Kural each day, from a fixed shuffled order of all 1330. Everyone sees the same Kural on the same day, and none repeats until all have been shown.
• Listen to the couplet and its meaning in your phone's Tamil voice.
• A reading streak, a calendar of the days you read, and an optional reminder at the time you choose.

LEARN BY HEART
• Recite the couplet as its words are hidden step by step, then check yourself.
• Spaced review brings each Kural back after 1, 2, 4, 7, 15, 30 and 60 days.
• A quiz: missing word, meaning, chapter and jumbled words.

BROWSE AND SEARCH
• All 133 chapters, arranged by book (பால்) and part (இயல்).
• Search in Tamil or English, by chapter name or by number.
• Save Kurals, and share them as an image or as text.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Paper, Palm leaf or Night pages, four accent colours, three fonts and adjustable text size.
• Read in Tamil, English or both.

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone, and the app works fully offline.

Kural Daily is open source: github.com/aatralabs/kural-daily
```

### Data safety

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
  Everything the app keeps (settings, reading history, streaks, learning, quiz scores, recent
  searches) stays on the phone and is never sent anywhere, so Google doesn't count it as
  collected.
- The form then asks nothing further. The listing will show *No data collected* and *No data
  shared with third parties*.

The app's manifest includes the `INTERNET` permission (React Native adds it). That's fine: the
answers are about what the app does, and it makes no network requests. If Play Console's App
bundle explorer shows `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` after the first upload, add it to
`android.blockedPermissions` in `app.json` and rebuild, so it matches the Advertising ID answer.

### Target audience

The texts suit all ages, and the app has no ads and collects no data. Choosing any age group under
13 brings the app under Google Play's Families policy, with stricter review. The app should meet
it, but the review may take longer. Choosing 13 and over is the simpler first launch, and younger
age groups can be added later.

## Before Production

New personal developer accounts must run a closed test with a minimum number of testers for a
minimum number of days before Production unlocks. Organisation accounts don't. Play Console's
dashboard shows whether this applies and the current numbers.
